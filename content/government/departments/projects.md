# Projects named in the national budget

These are appropriation lines whose text names Ocampo, Camarines Sur. An amount is what that year's national budget assigned to the line. It is not a report that the work was finished, and it is not the municipality's own project list.

The enacted figures come from the [BetterGov GAA search](https://budget.bettergov.ph/api/v1/gaa/search?q=Ocampo%2C%20Camarines%20Sur&limit=50), which reads the General Appropriations Act for 2020–2026. The search returned 37 lines. Amounts are in pesos.

The 2027 lines are from the House bill compared with the National Expenditure Program on [budget.bettergov.ph](https://budget.bettergov.ph). They are proposals, not an enacted budget.
