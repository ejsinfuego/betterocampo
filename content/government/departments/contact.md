## Public pages

These Facebook pages were read from public copies. The office directory above does not confirm them. Their wording is the page's own. It is not a separate proclamation. Phone numbers on these pages are not copied into the directory above when they differ from it. Ocampo CS Public Information is a community page, not an office account, so it is not listed.

- [Mswd Ocampo](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61587575925367) — Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office. The about line says it is that office's account.
- [Local Youth Development Office- Ocampo, Camarines Sur](https://www.facebook.com/277183935485458) — The page names Ms. Karen A. Benosa as LYDO-Designate. That name is not in the directory above.
- [Ocampo Tourism, Culture and the Arts Office](https://www.facebook.com/270889189434747) — Market Site, Poblacion West, Governor Jose T. Fuentebella National Highway, 4419. The listing shows Monday–Thursday, 7:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.
- [RHU OCAMPO](https://www.facebook.com/1278129182308012) — Rural health unit page. Its listed mobile matches the header RHU number, 9122516900. The directory row above keeps 0927-772-8320.
- [BFP R5 Ocampo](https://www.facebook.com/109157901666792) — Fire station page that says it is at the LGU compound. The header BFP number is 9615193815. The directory row above keeps 0943-827-5742.
- [BLGU Poblacion Central](https://www.facebook.com/113563351226873) — Barangay page for Poblacion Central. It lists La Purisima Street, weekday hours of 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and (054) 881-2096.

Public scrape, stated by the LGU. The LGU page reposted a CASURECO IV notice to buy land for a substation between Brgy. Hibago and Brgy. San Francisco. The deadline in that notice was 10 November 2026. The contact there is the cooperative in Tigaon, not the municipal Bids and Awards Committee.
