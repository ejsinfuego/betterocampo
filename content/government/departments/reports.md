# Municipality profile — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Ocampo is a 3rd-class landlocked municipality in Camarines Sur. The map point used for the town is 13.5635° N, 123.3724° E. Elevation is about 55.4 m. That elevation is approximate.

## Tourism host barangays

[Wikipedia — Ocampo, Camarines Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocampo,_Camarines_Sur) names Brgy. Santa Cruz for the Ocampo Deer Farm and Brgy. Hanawan for the Bamboo Farm. The map pins for both stay approximate until a GPS survey is checked. Murokburok Spring and Bicol Strawberry Farm do not get exact coordinates on this page.

## Left blank on purpose

Citizen's Charter fees, Municipal Revenue Code rates, garbage collection days, and Water District or electric-utility hotlines are not filled in here.

These [data.bettergov.ph](https://data.bettergov.ph) sets were not bulk-filtered into this guide. A row from them stays blank until it is checked:

- BLGF FY 2024 fiscal data
- Open PhilGEPS
- OpenHalalan v2.0
- DepEd BEIS
- Philippine Critical Facilities
- Barangay boundaries

The FY 2022 development-fund figures that were checked are on the [budget](/government/departments/budget) page. The map already draws barangay shapes from a separate public boundary file. That file was not replaced with the data.bettergov.ph boundary set.
