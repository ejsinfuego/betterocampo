# Municipal finances

These are the municipality-wide totals transcribed from Commission on Audit annual audit reports onto Wikidata. They are not a line-item budget, and this page does not restate the audit opinion. The 2024 report zip did not download from COA when this page was written (the site returned 403), so the figures below are the COA totals as recorded by Wikidata, not a fresh reading of the PDF.

| Item        | Amount (PHP)   |

| Expenditure | 172,901,049.13 |
| Assets      | 522,482,367.45 |

Source file: [Ocampo Annual Audit Report 2024 (ZIP)](https://www.coa.gov.ph/download/8385/camarines-sur/97367/ocampo-annual-audit-report-2024.zip). COA report index: [Annual Audit Reports, local government units](https://www.coa.gov.ph/reports/annual-audit-reports/aar-local-government-units/).

## What this is not

The Department of Budget and Management open data used by [budget.bettergov.ph](https://budget.bettergov.ph) covers the national budget. It does not publish Ocampo's municipal appropriation ordinance. National projects that name the town are on the Projects page.

PhilSys holds identity records. Those records are not an open dataset, and this portal does not publish them.
