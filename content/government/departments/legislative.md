# Sangguniang Bayan

The vice mayor presides over the Sangguniang Bayan. The names below are the officials listed for the current term. Vote totals for mayor and vice mayor come from the 2025 partial, unofficial Comelec media-server count published by Rappler. Council names are the list carried by Wikipedia from its DILG citation, and they match the 2025 council winners reported by PeoPlaid.

## Mayor and presiding officer

| Office     | Name                 | 2025 reported votes |
| ---------- | -------------------- | ------------------- |
| Mayor      | Ronald Allan J. Go   | 15,543              |
| Vice Mayor | Fidel R. Carido, Jr. | 12,148              |

Registered voters in 2025: 30,473, from the Comelec voter file cited on Wikidata.
