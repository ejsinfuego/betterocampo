# Request Access to Barangay Water Supply — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Public scrape, stated by the LGU. The [LGU Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/101706771551898) named the Ocampo Water District (OWADI) in a public advisory. The advisory said water rationing would run on 20 October 2026 because inflow to the reservoir was low. That notice was for one day. It did not include a hotline, a connection fee, or a list of barangays served.

Ask the water district or the municipal engineering office what a new connection requires. This page does not invent a rate or a schedule.

## Where to ask

- Your barangay hall
- Municipal Hall, Ocampo, Camarines Sur 4419
- [LGU Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/101706771551898)

No Water District hotline is listed here.
