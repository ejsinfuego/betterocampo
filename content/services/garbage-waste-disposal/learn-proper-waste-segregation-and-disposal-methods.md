# Proper Waste Segregation and Disposal — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Segregation in Ocampo follows Republic Act 9003, the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act. The municipal ordinance may add local rules and fines. Those peso amounts are not copied here.

## Separate these

| Category             | Examples                                                        | What to do                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Biodegradable        | Food scraps, peelings, garden waste                             | Green or labeled bin. Compost at home if you can.                                                           |
| Recyclable           | Bottles, glass, cans, paper, cartons, scrap metal               | Rinse and dry. Bring to a junk shop or ask the barangay where the materials recovery facility accepts them. |
| Residual             | Diapers, sachets, styrofoam, contaminated paper                 | Residual bin, on collection day only.                                                                       |
| Special or hazardous | Batteries, bulbs, paint, chemicals, electronics, medical sharps | Do not mix with household trash. Ask the barangay for a special collection.                                 |

## Rules that apply everywhere

- Do not burn waste.
- Do not put garbage out on a day the truck is not coming.
- Construction debris is not household waste. Ask the barangay who may haul it.
- Republic Act 9003 requires barangays to set up a materials recovery facility. Ask your barangay hall where theirs is, or where to bring recyclables if it is not yet operating.

## Businesses

Keep separate bins for customers and staff. Keep hauling receipts if you hire a private collector. Food businesses can ask the barangay whether kitchen waste can go to a composting area.

## Penalties

Illegal dumping and open burning can be penalized under RA 9003 and the municipal ordinance. Repeated violations can affect a business permit. Ask the municipal environment office for the schedule of fines instead of using another city's amounts.
