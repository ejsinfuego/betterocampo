# Request Special Collection for Hazardous Waste and Electronics — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Batteries, bulbs, paint, chemicals, and electronics do not belong in the regular garbage truck in Ocampo. The barangay and the municipal environment office schedule special collection. A standing weekly route is not published here.

## What to hold back from regular trash

| Kind                      | Examples                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Household hazardous waste | Batteries, fluorescent bulbs, paint, solvents, pesticides                                              |
| Electronics               | Phones, computers, printers, televisions, chargers                                                     |
| Household medical waste   | Syringes, sharps, heavily soiled dressings. Ask the barangay health station where to surrender sharps. |

## How to request pickup

1. Tell the barangay hall what you have, about how much, and your address.
2. Ask whether they will send the request to the municipal environment office or whether a drop-off day is already set.
3. Keep items in a closed, labeled box. Do not break bulbs or take devices apart.
4. Put the box out only when the barangay tells you to, or bring it to the drop-off they name.

A junk shop may accept some electronics. Ask the municipal environment office which collectors they recognize. Do not treat an unverified buyer as the municipal program.
