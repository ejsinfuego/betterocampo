# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid during Disasters — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Relief goods and cash aid in Ocampo are released through the barangay and the municipal offices below. This page does not list a pack, a peso amount, a payout date, or an evacuation center. Those change with each event, and the municipality has not published a standing schedule here.

Cash aid that is not disaster relief is posted on [Mswd Ocampo](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61587575925367). A 19 November 2026 post there announced an Uplift Program payout on 21 November 2026 at the Ocampo Cultural and Sports Complex in Poblacion West. Details are on the [family and community welfare](/services/social-welfare/get-family-and-community-welfare-support) page.

For a life-threatening emergency, call **911**. The header MDRRMO number, confirmed with the LGU, is 9475714857. The office directory lists 0915-919-9832 for that office on the [contact directory](/government/departments/contact).

## Where to go first

1. Go to your barangay hall, or send one household member if travel is unsafe. Give your full name, address, and how many people are in the house.
2. Ask whether the barangay is preparing a list for the municipal social welfare office, or whether a distribution is already set.
3. Follow the announcement on the [LGU Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/101706771551898). A post from another town is not an Ocampo distribution.

## Municipal offices

The Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) is headed by Eleanor C. Mangubat. Residents ask this office about family lists and about Department of Social Welfare and Development assistance that uses the municipal office as the venue.

The Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) is headed by Julius B. Celino. The municipal complex study places that office in Brgy. Hibago. It does not give a street address or a room number.

## National aid, not a local rate

The [Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_10121_2010.html) (Republic Act 10121) is the national framework for local disaster offices. Cash aid such as Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation is a Department of Social Welfare and Development program. Field Office V and the municipality announce when a payout is open. This page does not copy an amount from another city.
