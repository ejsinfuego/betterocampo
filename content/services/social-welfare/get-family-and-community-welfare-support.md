# Get Family and Community Welfare Support — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Barangay-level community projects and Indigenous Peoples communities are recorded in the sources below. This page does not list a cash amount, a schedule, or a hotline.

## Kalahi CIDSS

The International Journal of Research and Innovation in Social Science (June 2026) describes barangay-level Kalahi CIDSS implementation in Ocampo through:

- Barangay Representative Teams (BRTs)
- Barangay Sub-Project Management Committees (BSPMCs)
- Community bids and awards committees

The article is the source for those bodies. It is not used here to name a project, a barangay, or a cost. The municipal complex study also places a Kalahi CIDSS office in the town complex. That is an office location, not a project list.

## Indigenous Peoples communities

The NCIP 2022 Annual Accomplishment Report documents Indigenous Peoples communities under a Certificate of Ancestral Domain Title in the municipality. The community names, the CADT number, and the boundary are not in the note used for this page, so they stay blank.

The municipal complex study lists an Office of the Indigenous People in the town complex. That listing is not the CADT record.

## Announcement on the MSWDO page

The page [Mswd Ocampo](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61587575925367) describes itself as the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office account. On 19 November 2026 it announced an Uplift Program payout for the 3rd and 4th tranches:

- Date: 21 November 2026
- Time: 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
- Place: Ocampo Cultural and Sports Complex, Poblacion West

The post tells qualified beneficiaries to bring a valid national ID, three photocopies with three specimen signatures on each copy, and an active GCash number. It says active 4Ps beneficiaries do not need to attend. This is that post. It is not a standing schedule, and this page does not add a peso amount.
