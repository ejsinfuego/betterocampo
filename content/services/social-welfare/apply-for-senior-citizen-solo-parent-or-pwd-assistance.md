# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Senior citizen, solo parent, and person-with-disability IDs are issued through municipal offices in Ocampo. The municipality has not published a local cash amount, a fee, or its own document checklist on this portal. Ask the office below which papers it wants this month.

Phone numbers and emails for these offices are on the [contact directory](/government/departments/contact). They come from the LGU office directory and are not repeated here.

## Senior citizen ID

The Office of the Senior Citizens (OSCA) is headed by Salvacion T. Evora. The [Expanded Senior Citizens Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_9994_2010.html) (Republic Act 9994) covers Filipino residents who are 60 or older. OSCA is the office that issues the senior citizen ID. National discounts and privileges are in that law. This page does not add a local cash grant.

The municipal complex study places the Senior Citizen's Office in the town complex. It does not give a room number.

## Solo parent ID

The Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) is headed by Eleanor C. Mangubat. The [Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11861_2022.html) (Republic Act 11861) is the national law for the solo parent ID. Ask MSWDO whether Ocampo is issuing IDs and what proof of status it requires. A local subsidy amount is not published here.

## Person with disability ID

The Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) is headed by Edwin G. Vales. The [Magna Carta for Disabled Persons](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1992/ra_7277_1992.html) (Republic Act 7277) and [Republic Act 10754](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2016/ra_10754_2016.html) set the national ID and discount rules. Ask PDAO which medical certificate it accepts. This page does not list a local benefit amount.
