# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Stall rental in a public market is handled by the Municipality of Ocampo. This portal does not yet have the market's stall map, rental rates, or application form.

Ask the municipal treasurer or the market office at the Municipal Hall, Ocampo, Camarines Sur 4419, and check announcements on the [LGU Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/101706771551898).

When you inquire, ask for:

- Who may apply, and whether residents are prioritized
- The current monthly rate and any deposit
- The lease length and the rules on transferring a stall
- Required IDs, barangay clearance, and a mayor's permit if you will sell from the stall

Do not pay a reservation fee to anyone who cannot issue an official receipt from the municipality.
