# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos, or Tourism Promotions — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Ocampo businesses can join fairs and promotions run by the municipality, the barangay, the provincial government, or national agencies. This page does not list a current Ocampo event calendar. Watch the [LGU Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/101706771551898) and ask the mayor's office or the business permits office when a call opens.

## Who usually joins

- Micro, small, and medium enterprises
- Cooperatives and livelihood associations
- Tourism and food enterprises, when the event asks for them
- Makers of local products, including OTOP-style goods

Organizers decide eligibility. Registration with DTI, SEC, or CDA and a current mayor's permit are the usual starting point.

## How to apply when a call is open

1. Prepare DTI, SEC, or CDA registration, BIR Form 2303, and the current mayor's permit.
2. Add product compliance when it applies, such as an FDA license for processed food.
3. Submit the organizer's form with a product list, photos, and booth needs.
4. Pay a booth fee only if the written guidelines say there is one. Do not assume a fee from another town applies in Ocampo.
5. Follow ingress, electrical, fire, and waste rules on the event days.

## What to budget for

Treat these as your own planning items, not as municipal fees:

- Booth fee, if the organizer charges one
- Tarpaulin, labels, and price cards
- Samples, staff meals, and transport
- A small contingency

## After the event

Write down leads, orders, and what sold. Follow up within a few days. Keep receipts and the event guidelines with your business records.

## Sample inquiry

> Subject: Booth inquiry for [event name]
>
> Hello. We are an Ocampo, Camarines Sur business offering [product]. We would like to join [event] on [date]. Attached are our registration, product list, and photos. Thank you.
