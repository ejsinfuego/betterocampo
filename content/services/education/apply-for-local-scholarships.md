# Apply for Local Scholarships — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

The Municipality of Ocampo has not published a scholarship call, a list of partner schools, or a coverage amount on this portal. When the municipality or the district representative opens slots, the notice should come from the [LGU Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/101706771551898) or the Municipal Hall.

Residents can still apply to national programs on their own.

## National programs to check

| Program                                           | Who it is for                                                   | Where to start                              |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| CHED scholarship programs                         | College students who meet the current academic and income rules | [ched.gov.ph](https://ched.gov.ph/)         |
| DOST-SEI undergraduate scholarships               | Grade 12 students entering a science or technology course       | [sei.dost.gov.ph](https://sei.dost.gov.ph/) |
| OWWA scholarships                                 | Qualified dependents of active OWWA members                     | [owwa.gov.ph](https://owwa.gov.ph/)         |
| UniFAST tertiary subsidy (TES and related grants) | Students in participating higher education institutions         | [unifast.gov.ph](https://unifast.gov.ph/)   |

Coverage and deadlines change every cycle. Read the current call. Do not use an old announcement from another city.

## Papers that are often required

- Barangay certificate of residency in Ocampo
- PSA birth certificate
- Report card or grades
- Certificate of good moral character
- Proof of family income, or a certificate of indigency if the program asks for it
- Photos and a school ID or proof of admission

## How to apply

1. Choose a program that is actually open.
2. Save the eligibility rules and the deadline.
3. Request barangay and school certificates early.
4. File only through the link or window named in that call.
5. If you are accepted, keep the grade requirement and renew each term.
