# Supplementary Activities for Schools — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

Clubs, contests, and scouting in Ocampo start at the school. Division-wide events are announced by the Schools Division of Camarines Sur through the school, not by a city hall in another province. This page does not list a municipal library schedule.

## Where to look

1. The school's bulletin or official social media page.
2. The adviser for the club or the MAPEH, science, or TLE department.
3. DepEd orders that apply in every division, linked below.

Schools also field science, arts, ICT, and sports groups. Tryouts are posted by the school.

## Alternative learning

The Alternative Learning System is for learners who are out of school or need a flexible schedule. Ask the nearest public school who the ALS teacher is for Ocampo.

## How to join

1. Check the school's club fair or adviser list.
2. Bring a school ID and a parent consent form if the adviser requires one.
3. Note the meeting time and any safety rules.
4. Ask whether the activity charges a fee. Many do not.

## For parents

Ask who supervises the activity, when it ends, and how learners get home. Keep academics and activities on a weekly schedule the learner can sustain.
