# Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement — Ocampo, Camarines Sur

This portal does not yet name a municipal hospital, its bed count, or a phone number for Ocampo. For emergencies, go to the nearest emergency room. For a planned admission, ask the Municipal Health Office where residents are referred.

## Emergency

1. Go to the emergency room. Do not wait for an appointment.
2. Staff will triage and take initial orders.
3. If you are admitted, sign the consent, then submit any ID, PhilHealth papers, or HMO letter you have. Missing papers can usually be followed up by a relative.

## Planned confinement

1. Get the admitting order from the physician.
2. Bring a valid ID, PhilHealth ID and Member Data Record, and the HMO or company guarantee letter if you have one.
3. Complete admission and billing before you go to the room.

## What to bring

- A photo ID
- PhilHealth ID and Member Data Record, or proof that you are a dependent
- The doctor's referral, for a planned admission
- Personal items for a stay

PhilHealth benefits and No Balance Billing depend on membership category and on whether the facility is a government hospital accredited for that package. Confirm both at the billing desk. This page does not post room rates.
